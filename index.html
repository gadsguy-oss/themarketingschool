Questions, answered.

Which package is right for me? +
If you want to learn AI for ads on your own, start with AI Ads Training at $297/mo. If you're spending under $5k a month and want help running it, start with Teaching: we manage one channel for you and teach you the rest. If you're spending $5k–$30k a month and want us running the account with you — management, creative, dashboard, weekly sessions — the Growth Club is your fit. If you're spending $30k+ and want the whole operation handled, All-In is the move. Not sure? Take the free 3-minute diagnosis and we'll point you to the right one.
Can I upgrade between packages? +
Yes. Most members move up as they see results. We'll credit any unused portion of your current plan toward the upgrade.
Do I need a minimum ad spend? +
For Teaching: no minimum. For the Growth Club: we recommend at least $5k/mo so there's enough data to optimize. For All-In: we generally only take on accounts spending $30k/mo or more.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Teaching cancels anytime before your next billing date. The Growth Club and All-In start with a 90-day initial term, billed monthly. After that, everything is month-to-month.
What if I don't see results? +
We're honest about what we can and can't control. Creative and media buying are huge levers, but they're not the only ones — your product, offer, and funnel matter too. If after 90 days we haven't moved the needle, we'll have a real conversation about why and what to do next.
Who runs the program? +
A team of operators with deep experience managing paid media for major brands. You'll get real eyes on your account from people who've spent eight figures of their own clients' money.
