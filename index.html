How we find the leaks and fix them fast

AI does the digging. Twenty years of running ads decides what to do about it.

Step 1

Spy on what's winning

Our Grok Bot scans the Meta Ad Library for competitor ads that have run for months, the ones that are actually paying for themselves. You start from proven angles, not guesses.

Step 2

Audit every account

Claude connects to your Google and Meta accounts and reads the search terms, placements, budgets, and tracking. Wasted spend and broken conversions get flagged in hours, not weeks.

Step 3

Fix what matters first

Evan reviews every finding and decides what's worth changing. Then the fixes go live: negative keywords, budget shifts, new ads, landing page changes.

Step 4

Keep watching

The AI keeps checking your accounts after launch, so new leaks get caught early instead of showing up on next month's bill.

AI finds problems fast, but it doesn't know which ones matter. That's the part experience handles. Every recommendation is checked by someone who has managed $200M+ in ad spend.
