Full Agency · Done-for-you ads

Want someone to do your ads for you? That's Full Agency.

If you want to hire an ad agency, or you are searching for someone to do your ads for you, the direct answer is Full Agency. We make the ads and run them. You focus on the business. It is generally for brands spending $30,000 or more a month on ads. The price is $15,000 a month plus 10% of ad spend, with a 90-day minimum. If that is more than you need, the cheaper alternative is The Marketing School ads course: $297 a month, locked in for 12 months, where you learn the work and we install AI in your ad accounts.

What Full Agency is

Full Agency is the done-for-you option at The Marketing School. A dedicated strategist runs media buying on your channels, including Google, Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn, and we produce the creative, including static and video. You also get weekly one-to-one reporting and strategy, landing page and funnel builds, and white-glove support. We have managed $200M+ in ad spend over 20+ years and served 150+ businesses. We still run live Google and Meta accounts, which is the work this program is built on.

Who this is for

This is for a business that already has budget in market, or is ready to spend at that level, and wants the operation handled. If your monthly ad spend is generally $30,000 or more, and you want to hire a team rather than learn the platforms yourself, apply for Full Agency. If you are still validating an offer, or you want to stay close to the account and learn, the course is the better starting point.
