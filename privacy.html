Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who we are

The Marketing School ("we," "us") runs themarketingschool.com and offers paid-advertising education, done-for-you creative, and ad management services. Questions about this policy go to evan@themarketingschool.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you take the Ad Account Score diagnosis, fill out a package or contact form, or email us: your name, email, phone number, website, company, monthly ad spend, the package you're interested in, how you'd like to be contacted, and anything you write in a message.
  • Your diagnosis answers. The answers you choose in the quiz and the score and recommendation they produce.
  • Campaign and referral details. If you arrive from an ad or link, we may record the page you came from and campaign tags in the link (such as UTM parameters, gclid, or fbclid).
  • Usage data. Like most websites, we and our analytics and advertising partners may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and how our ads perform. This can include your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and actions taken (for example, completing the diagnosis).

How we use it

  • To show you your diagnosis results and recommend a package.
  • To contact you the way you asked — by email, phone call, or text message — about your inquiry and our services.
  • To deliver services you sign up for and to run our business (records, billing, support).
  • To measure and improve our website, offers, and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number and choose text or call as your preferred contact method, you agree that we may contact you at that number about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text or telling us on a call or by email, and we'll stop.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and our business, and only as needed for that work. These include:

  • Google (Google Forms, Google Sheets, and Gmail to store and respond to inquiries; Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure site and ad performance).
  • Meta (the Meta Pixel, to measure the performance of our Facebook and Instagram ads).
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business.

Cookies and advertising choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, and manage Meta ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings. Industry opt-outs are also available at optout.aboutads.info.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and lead information for as long as it's useful for following up and running our business, and client information for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights under local law. Email evan@themarketingschool.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time. We won't treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Email evan@themarketingschool.com or use our contact page.
