Can AI run my Google Ads?

AI can help with Google Ads, but it cannot fully run the account on its own. It is useful for writing ads, spotting wasted spend, and reporting results. It cannot replace a clear offer, working conversion tracking, or a human who reviews what the account is actually doing. If a tool promises to "set it and forget it," be skeptical. The Marketing School installs AI in your ad accounts and teaches you how to run Google Ads — and Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn — with that help in place.

What AI can do in a Google Ads account

AI is fast at drafts and at reading large sets of terms and results. In this course, the done-for-you install is there to help find wasted spend, write ads, and report results. The AI ad prompt pack is included so you can produce better ads in minutes. That can save time. It is not the same thing as an autopilot media buyer.

What AI cannot do

AI does not know your customer the way you do. It cannot invent a winning offer. It cannot fix a landing page that does not convert, or a pixel that is missing. It will not take responsibility for budget mistakes. A client went from $0 to $30k in profitable revenue with Google Ads; that took a real account, a real offer, and human judgment, not a bot left alone overnight.
