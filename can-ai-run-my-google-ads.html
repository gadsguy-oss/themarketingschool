Guide · AI and Google Ads

Can AI run my Google Ads?

Not by itself. AI is great at finding wasted spend, writing ad drafts, and turning numbers into reports. It can't fix a weak offer or broken tracking, and left alone it can spend money fast on the wrong things. The setup that works is AI doing the digging and a human making the calls.

What AI does well in Google Ads

Finding wasted spend in search terms, drafting ads and variations, spotting trends in your numbers, and turning it all into a report you can read in two minutes.

Where AI goes wrong

Automated campaigns optimize toward whatever you tell them to. If tracking counts the wrong action, AI scales the wrong thing. It also can't tell you that your offer or landing page is the real problem.
