Questions, answered

How much does it cost?

What's the difference between Done-For-You Ads and AI Implementation?

Done-For-You Ads: we run your ads for you, with AI built in, from $297 a month. AI Implementation: we set up AI in your marketing and ad accounts for your team to use, with custom pricing scoped on a call.

No. AI is fast at finding waste, drafting ads, and reporting, but it can't fix a weak offer or broken tracking, and it shouldn't run unchecked. A human makes the calls. More on whether AI can run Google Ads.
