The Dual Agent Build Pack · 12 live 1-on-1 sessions

Build your website or app with AI — in live 1-on-1 sessions.

If you want to build a website with AI, or build an app with AI, and you want a person with you while you do it, the Dual Agent Build Pack is that help. It is not a course, and it is not a do-it-yourself tool you are left alone with. It is 12 live one-on-one build sessions. In each session you use the dual agent method: one AI plans, writes, and reviews; a second AI builds; you approve each step. You can use it to build websites, apps, landing pages, and other projects. It is for small business owners, or anyone starting a business. No developer or coding needed.

How the dual agent method works

Most people get stuck when they ask one AI to invent the plan and also produce the work in the same breath. This method splits the job. The first AI plans, writes, and reviews. The second AI builds from that plan. You approve each step before the work moves on. That is the same way real projects get built: a plan, a build, a review, then the next step. You stay in control. You do not need to write code, and you do not hand the project to a developer.

What you can build

Use the sessions to build a website, a landing page, an app, or another project your business needs. The point of AI website builder coaching here is not a template you click through alone. It is live 1-on-1 time, on your actual project, with the method in use. Bring a new business idea or an existing small business; both are a fit.

What you get

You get 12 live 1-on-1 build sessions. That is the pack. There is no separate course to sit through first. Sessions are used to plan, build, review, and keep going until the project moves. If you pay monthly, you get one session a month for 12 months. If you pay up front, you can book the 12 sessions at your own pace, even weekly, and finish in about 3 months.
