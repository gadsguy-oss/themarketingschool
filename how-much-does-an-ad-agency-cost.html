How much does an ad agency cost?

There's no single price. Agency fees depend on how many platforms you're on, who makes the creative, and how much you spend. Here's what we charge: Done-For-You Ads is $297 a month with a 12-month price lock, or $2,497 up front, for 1 or 2 platforms and accounts spending under $10k a month. Full Agency has custom pricing for accounts spending $30k+ a month.

What usually drives the price

The number of platforms, who makes the creative, how often the account gets worked, how much reporting you get, and how much you spend. Some agencies charge a flat fee, some a percentage of ad spend, some both. Ask any agency which one it is and exactly what's included.
