How much does an ad agency cost?

There is no single price for an ad agency. Fees vary with scope, channels, creative, and the size of the ad account. Some businesses hire a freelancer for a narrow slice of work; others hire a full-service team to make the ads, run them, and report every week. The only prices we can state as fact are our own.

How agency fees usually work (qualitative, not a market study)

Most paid-ads help is billed as a monthly retainer, sometimes plus a percentage of ad spend, and sometimes with project fees for creative or landing pages. Contracts often have a minimum term. What you pay should be read next to what is included — buying only, or creative, funnels, and reporting too — and next to how much you are already spending on ads. Those patterns are typical in the market; they are not a survey and not a promise of what any unnamed agency charges.

How to think about the number

Compare the fee to your monthly ad spend and to the work you actually need. A smaller account can be a poor fit for a large retainer. A large account can waste more on leaks than it would spend on serious help.
