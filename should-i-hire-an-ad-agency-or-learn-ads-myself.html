Should I hire an ad agency or run ads myself?

If you have more money than time, hand your ads to someone who does this every day. If you have more time than money and you like being in the account, doing it yourself can work, but expect some wasted spend while you figure it out.

Book a free account review

When to hand it off

You're busy running the business, the account has stalled, or you can't tell what your ad spend is buying. Done-For-You Ads runs 1 or 2 platforms for you, with AI built in.

When doing it yourself makes sense

You have the time, a small budget you're happy to test with, and you want to be hands-on. If that's you, AI Implementation can set up AI in your accounts so you're not doing it all by hand.

When you need a full agency

You spend $30k+ a month and want creative, buying, funnels, and reporting owned by a team. That's Full Agency, with custom pricing scoped on a call.
