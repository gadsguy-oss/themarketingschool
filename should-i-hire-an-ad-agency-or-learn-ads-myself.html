Should I hire an ad agency or learn ads myself?

You should hire an ad agency when you want the work fully handled and your spend is high enough to support that kind of retainer. You should learn ads yourself when you want to keep control, understand the account, and spend less on help. There is no single right answer for every business. At The Marketing School, those two paths are Full Agency (we run it) and the ads course (you learn, and we install AI in your accounts).

Learn ads yourself if you are close to the customer, you want to understand where the money goes, or you are not ready for a full-agency retainer. When hiring an agency is the better move

Hire someone to run your ads if you already know the business will spend enough, you do not want to be in the account every week, and you want creative, buying, and reporting owned by a team. Questions, answered

Should a small business hire an ad agency?

Only if you want the work fully handled and the retainer makes sense next to your spend. Many businesses are better off learning ads first. Our course is $297 a month for 12 months; Full Agency is generally for $30k+/mo spend.

Can I learn ads and still get help?

Yes. The course includes a done-for-you AI install and a monthly account review. It is not the same as Full Agency running the account for you.

What if I want to switch later?

You can apply for Full Agency later if the done-for-you path becomes the right fit. The course itself is a 12-month commitment at $297 a month, or a year paid up front at $2,497.
