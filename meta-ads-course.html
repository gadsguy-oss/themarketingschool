What you will learn in Meta ads

You learn how to structure the account, brief and test creative, use AI to write ads and variations, and read results without poking the account every day. Creative fatigue and weak follow-up are common reasons Meta spend stops working. The course covers the ads and the habits around them. Optional ad creative (we make the images and videos) and CRM setup (so leads get followed up) are available; ask about pricing.

How AI helps a Meta account

We connect AI to your ad accounts to help find wasted spend, write ads, and report results. AI will not save a weak offer or missing tracking. A monthly account review and members-only weekly classes (Tuesday lead gen and Friday ads traffic) keep a human in the loop.
