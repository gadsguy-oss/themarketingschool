What you will learn

You learn how to set up a LinkedIn campaign the right way, write ads for a professional audience, test without lighting budget on the wrong audience, and read results against a real cost per lead or customer. LinkedIn can be expensive when the offer or the audience is loose. The course treats that as a craft you can learn, not a mystery. Optional CRM setup (ask about pricing) exists because a LinkedIn lead that nobody follows up is wasted spend.

AI and the monthly review

We connect AI to your ad accounts so it can help write ads, surface waste, and report results. A monthly account review is included.
